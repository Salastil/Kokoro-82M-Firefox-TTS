Kokoro Reader

Reads articles and selected text aloud using Kokoro-82M, entirely on-device via WebAssembly. All synthesis happens on your CPU in a background worker -- no text or audio is ever sent anywhere. The only network access is a one-time download of the model weights from Hugging Face, which the browser caches locally.

Voice & speed

Model quality

Changing this downloads a different set of model weights the next time you read something. Larger = better quality & slower on CPU.

Performance

Synthesis runs on your CPU by default, which is what this extension is designed and tested for. GPU acceleration (WebGPU) is experimental and opt-in -- if your browser or system doesn't support it, Kokoro Reader automatically falls back to CPU.
